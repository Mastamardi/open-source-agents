The Problem in Bengaluru

💸

High Deposits

Several months of rent requested upfront as a major financial burden.

🚨

Rental Scams

Fake house listings asking for advance money before property visits.

❓

Lack of Info

Zero transparency on landlord deposit return habits or hidden charges.

🎤 Script:
“There are three major problems people face when renting a house in Bengaluru.

First, high deposits. Some landlords ask tenants to pay several months of rent as a deposit. This can be a big financial burden.

Second, rental scams. Some people post fake house listings and ask for advance money before showing the property.

Third, lack of information. Before renting, we often don’t know whether the landlord returns the deposit properly, maintains the property, or has hidden charges.

These problems can cost tenants both money and peace of mind. That’s why we created RentRakshak.”
Slide 3

Our Solution

🔍

Listing Analysis

Paste ads to check for suspicious details and unusual deposits.

📊

Rent Comparison

Compare requested rent with neighborhood benchmarks.

👥

Tenant Community

Share and read anonymous rental experiences safely.

🎤 Script:
“RentRakshak helps tenants in three main ways.

First, listing analysis. Users can paste a rental advertisement, and our system checks for suspicious details, unusual deposits, and other warning signs.

Second, rent comparison. Users can compare the requested rent with available neighborhood benchmarks to understand whether the price seems reasonable.

Third, anonymous tenant community. Tenants can share their rental experiences without publicly revealing their identity, helping other people make better decisions.

In simple words, RentRakshak helps people check before they trust and pay.”

🎤 Script:
“We used different technologies to build RentRakshak.
- Python handles the main logic and checks rental information.
- Google ADK helps us build an AI agent that can understand and respond to rental-related questions.
- Streamlit helps us create the user interface — the screens people interact with.
- SQLite helps us store information in a database.
We combine predefined rules with AI. Rules help us check specific conditions consistently, while AI helps us understand more complex questions.

This makes our platform useful for both quick checks and interactive assistance.”
