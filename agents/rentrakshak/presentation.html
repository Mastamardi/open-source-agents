Slide 2 · The Problem

The 3 Major Rental Traps

💸

1. High Deposits

Landlords demanding massive multi-month deposits, creating severe financial burdens for newcomers.

🚨

2. Advance Scams

Fake listings demanding 'visiting fees' or UPI token money before even showing the property.

🤐

3. Zero Info

No transparency on deposit refund histories, maintenance quality, or hidden landlord clauses.
